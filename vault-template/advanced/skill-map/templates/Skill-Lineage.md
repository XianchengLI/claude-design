# Skill Lineage — Replacements & Retirements

Succession record for skills that WERE installed and later replaced, merged, or retired. When a
"new" candidate turns out to be something recorded here (or a fork/descendant of it), recognize
it instantly instead of re-evaluating from scratch. Complements Candidate-Skills (evaluated but
never installed). A retired skill's standalone inventory note is DELETED — this file is its
permanent record; retired skills must not linger as standalone graph nodes.

| Retired skill (origin) | Replaced by | Date | Why | Remnants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| example-old-skill (source repo + version) | successor-skill (repo @ commit) | date | One-line verdict from the content diff | Backup path; reference sites updated |

Recognition notes:

- Record lineage sprawl here (e.g. "any future skill named X is likely a sibling of this
  family — check against the installed successor before evaluating as new").
